## Characterization status

The author reports university-led fabrication and optical characterization. Numerical measured stopband center, rejection ratio, and bandwidth have **not yet been extracted from publicly archived raw spectra**. Transmission alone cannot be treated as absolute reflectivity without an adequate calibrated power budget.
